Track record

The company is new. The work is not.

VARGERSOFT is the software division of VARGER Group LLC, Conroe, Texas: engineers with 24 years in operations who build AI agents, apps and websites.

We built the software we could not buy

For years we bought business software for our own companies, and for years we ended up running the business from a spreadsheet next to it. Then we noticed the same spreadsheet on every other owner's desk: hardware, parts, transportation, manufacturing. Small and mid-size businesses are sold enterprise software with features removed and the price barely reduced. VARGERSOFT exists to close that gap.

Anyone can write code now. Knowing what to build is the hard part.

AI made software cheap to produce. It did not make it easier to know which step of a process is actually costing the money. That takes having signed the payroll, chased the missing part and closed a month that did not add up. We use AI as a tool and bring the operating judgment ourselves.

Where the experience comes from

VARGER Group LLC was formed in Texas in 2026. The team behind it has engineered processes and run operating businesses since 2002: eight years in automotive quality engineering on OEM programs for GM, Ford, Volvo, Nissan and Stellantis, and more than fifteen years building and running retail, manufacturing and service companies.
