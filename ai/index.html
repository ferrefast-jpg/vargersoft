FAQ

Straight answers

How much does an AI agent cost?

Each agent has a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee. The Website AI Assistant is $850 setup plus $179 a month. The AI Voice Receptionist is $1,950 setup plus $349 a month with 500 minutes included. Every price is on the AI Agents page.

How long does setup take?

Seven business days for an AI agent, counted from the day we receive your business information and access to your calendar or phone line. Websites and custom software are quoted with their own timeline.

Do the agents really work in Spanish?

Yes. Every agent works in English and Spanish. The phone receptionist detects the language the caller speaks and answers in it, and the chat assistant switches when the visitor does.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Service is month to month and you can cancel at any time. The setup fee is paid once.

Who owns my data?

You do. Call summaries, conversations, leads and content belong to your business, and you can export them when you ask. We do not sell customer data.

Will my customers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The agents introduce themselves as virtual assistants. They sound natural, but we do not present them as people, and they transfer to a person when the customer asks.

What happens when the agent does not know an answer?

It says so, takes the customer's details and sends them to you. The agents answer from information you approve and do not invent prices or policies.

Can you work with the phone number and website I already have?

Yes. You keep your number and forward calls to the agent. The chat assistant installs on WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Hostinger and custom sites.
