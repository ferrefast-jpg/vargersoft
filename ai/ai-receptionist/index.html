The problem

A missed call is usually a customer who calls the next business on the list. Most small businesses miss calls at lunch, after hours and whenever the counter is busy. And when the caller speaks Spanish and nobody on shift does, the call ends in thirty seconds.

What it does

  • Answers every call, day or night, with a natural human-sounding voice.
  • Detects whether the caller speaks English or Spanish and answers in that language.
  • Books, confirms and reschedules appointments on your calendar.
  • Answers common questions: hours, location, services, prices you approve.
  • Transfers to a person when the caller asks or the situation needs it.
  • Emails you a written summary of every call.

What's included

  • A new phone number, or your current number forwarded to the agent.
  • Your choice of voice and a bilingual script written around your business.
  • Calendar connection and call-transfer rules.
  • 500 minutes of calls every month. Extra minutes at $0.35.
  • Monthly review of call summaries and script adjustments.

Questions

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The agent says it is a virtual assistant at the start of the call. It sounds natural, but we do not present it as a person.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. You forward your number to the agent, either always or only when you do not pick up.

What happens when the agent cannot answer a question?

It takes the message, tells the caller when to expect a call back and emails you the details. It does not make up an answer.

How long does setup take?

Seven business days from the day we receive your business information and calendar access.
