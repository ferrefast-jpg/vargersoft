Appointment Agent

A full calendar, without the phone tag.

Setup in 7 business days English / Español

Start setupBook a free 20-min call

The problem

Scheduling eats the day in small pieces: a call to book, a call to confirm, a call to move it. Every appointment nobody confirmed is an empty slot you already paid staff to cover.

What it does

  • Books appointments by phone and text against your real availability.
  • Sends a confirmation and a reminder before each appointment.
  • Lets the customer reschedule or cancel without calling the front desk.
  • Offers a freed slot to the next customer on your waiting list.

What's included

  • Connection to Google Calendar, Outlook or your booking system.
  • Bilingual confirmation and reminder messages.
  • Text-messaging registration with the phone carriers, handled for you.
  • Monthly report: booked, confirmed, rescheduled and missed.

Questions

Does it replace my booking system?

No. It works on top of the calendar or booking system you already use.

Do customers need an app?

No. They answer a call or reply to a text.

Who can it text?

Customers who gave you their number and agreed to be contacted. Every message includes a way to opt out.
