The problem

Most visitors leave a website with a question they never asked. A contact form asks them to wait a day for an answer. By then they have already called someone else.

What it does

  • Answers questions from your own approved information, not from guesses.
  • Captures name, phone and what the visitor needs, and sends it to you right away.
  • Gives quotes from the price rules you set.
  • Books appointments on your calendar.
  • Works in English and Spanish and switches when the visitor does.

What's included

Questions

Does it work on any website?

Yes. It installs with one snippet of code on WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Hostinger and custom sites.

Where do the answers come from?

From the information you approve. When a question falls outside it, the assistant offers to take the visitor's contact details instead.

Who owns the conversations?

You do. You can export them whenever you ask.
