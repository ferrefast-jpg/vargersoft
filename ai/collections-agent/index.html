Payment Reminder Agent

Invoices get paid sooner when someone remembers to ask.

Setup in 7 business days English / Español

The problem

Nobody likes calling a good customer about a late invoice, so the call does not happen. The invoice ages, the cash is tied up, and the awkward conversation only gets harder.

What it does

  • Sends a friendly reminder before the due date and on the due date.
  • Follows a schedule you approve after the due date: email, then text, then a call.
  • Includes the payment link so the customer can pay on the spot.
  • Records promises to pay and tells you when one is missed.

What's included

  • Reminder schedule and wording written with you, in both languages.
  • Connection to your invoicing system or a spreadsheet of open invoices.
  • Monthly report: reminders sent, payments received and open balances.

Questions

Is this a collections agency?

No. The agent reminds your own customers, in your business name, about invoices you issued. It does not buy or collect debts for others.

Can I control the tone?

Yes. You approve every message and the full schedule before anything is sent.

Can a customer stop the reminders?

Yes. Every text and email has an opt-out, and you can pause any account yourself.
