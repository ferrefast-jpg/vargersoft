SEO vs. AEO vs. GEO: what each one means

SEO, search engine optimization, helps your pages rank in search results. AEO, answer engine optimization, shapes your content so a search engine or assistant can lift a direct answer from it. GEO, generative engine optimization, helps AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity read, trust and cite your business when they write an answer. The three share one foundation: pages a machine can read and content that answers real questions.

Last reviewed: November 17, 2026 · VARGERSOFT

SEOAEOGEO
Where you appearThe list of search resultsThe direct answer boxInside an AI-written answer
What it rewardsRelevant, trusted pagesShort, clear answers to a questionFacts that are easy to read and verify
Typical workTitles, speed, links, contentQuestion headings, FAQ pagesStructured data, consistent business facts

What should a small business do first?

  • Make sure each page arrives as complete HTML, with its own title and description.
  • Write one page per service, and answer the main question in the first two sentences.
  • Publish your prices, hours, service area and contact details in text, not only in images.
  • Add structured data for your business, services and FAQs.
  • Keep your name, address and phone identical everywhere they appear.

What nobody can promise

No one controls what a search engine ranks or what an AI assistant says. Honest work removes the technical reasons you are ignored and measures the result over time. Be careful with anyone who guarantees a first position or a recommendation.
