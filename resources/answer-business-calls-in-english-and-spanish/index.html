How to answer business calls in English and Spanish without hiring

The simplest way to answer calls in both languages without hiring is an AI receptionist that detects the caller's language and answers in it. The caller does not press a button or wait for a bilingual employee. Other options are a phone menu, a bilingual answering service, or forwarding Spanish calls to one bilingual person.

Last reviewed: November 17, 2026 · VARGERSOFT

Option 1: a phone menu

"For English press 1, para español oprima 2." It is cheap and familiar. Its weakness is what happens after the button: someone still has to answer in that language.

Option 2: one bilingual employee

It works until that person is at lunch, on another call or on vacation. The business then answers in one language again.

Option 3: a bilingual answering service

A person answers and takes a message in either language. Ask whether Spanish is covered at every hour or only during certain shifts.

Option 4: an AI receptionist

The agent listens to the first words, recognizes the language and continues in it. It can book, answer questions and take messages, and it sends you the summary in the language you choose.

What to check before you choose

  • Call your own number after hours and see what a customer hears.
  • Ask a Spanish speaker to test any service with a real question.
  • Make sure written follow-ups, such as texts and emails, are bilingual too.
