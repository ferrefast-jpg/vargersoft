How to prepare your website for AI agents

To prepare a website for AI agents, make every page readable without running scripts, state your services, prices, hours and location in plain text, add structured data, allow AI crawlers in robots.txt, and give agents a clear way to act, such as a simple form or an MCP server. Most of this is the same work that helps search engines and people.

Last reviewed: November 17, 2026 · VARGERSOFT

Step 1: check what a machine sees

Open a page, choose "View page source" and search for a sentence you can read on screen. If it is missing, the content is added later by scripts, and many crawlers will never see it.

Step 2: say the facts in text

Agents compare businesses on facts: what you do, where, when, and for how much. Put them in sentences and tables. A price that only appears inside an image does not exist for a machine.

Step 3: add structured data

Structured data is a labeled copy of the same facts in a format machines read without guessing: this is the business name, this is a service, this is its price. It is invisible to visitors and useful to every crawler.

Step 4: open the door

Check robots.txt. If it blocks AI crawlers, assistants cannot use your pages in their answers. Allow the ones you want and keep a sitemap that lists every page.

Step 5: make the action simple

An agent that wants to book or ask for a quote needs a short path: a form with clear labels, or tools published through an MCP server. Decide which requests can be confirmed automatically and which a person approves.
