How much does an AI receptionist cost for a small business?

An AI receptionist is usually paid in two parts: a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee that includes a block of call minutes. At VARGERSOFT the AI Voice Receptionist costs $1,950 to set up and $349 a month with 500 minutes included, and $0.35 for each extra minute.

Last reviewed: November 17, 2026 · VARGERSOFT

What the setup fee pays for

Setup is the work of making the agent yours: writing the script around your services and prices, choosing the voice, connecting your calendar, setting the rules for when a call goes to a person, and testing it with real scenarios in both languages.

What the monthly fee pays for

The monthly fee covers the phone line, the voice and AI usage for your included minutes, the call summaries, and the monthly review where the script is adjusted from real calls.

How to estimate your minutes

Count the calls you get in a normal week and multiply by the length of a typical call. A shop with 40 calls a week at about three minutes each uses roughly 480 minutes a month. If you do not know your numbers, your phone bill or carrier portal shows them.

Questions to ask any provider

  • Is there a contract, or is it month to month?
  • What happens when I pass my included minutes?
  • Does it really answer in Spanish, or only read a translated script?
  • Who owns the call summaries and recordings?
  • Can it transfer to a person, and can I keep my number?
