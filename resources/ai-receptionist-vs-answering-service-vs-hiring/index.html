AI receptionist vs. answering service vs. hiring: which fits?

An AI receptionist fits when most calls are routine: hours, prices, booking and messages. It answers at any hour in English and Spanish. A live answering service fits when callers need a person but not your person. A hired receptionist fits when the job is much more than the phone. Many businesses combine an AI receptionist with staff who take the transferred calls.

Where an AI receptionist is the wrong choice

When most calls are emotional, complex or negotiated, a person should answer. The AI is still useful after hours and for overflow, but it should not be the only voice a caller can reach.

A simple way to decide

Write down the last twenty calls and mark each one as routine or not. If fifteen or more are routine, an AI receptionist will carry most of the load and your staff keep the rest.
