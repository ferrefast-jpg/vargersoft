What is an AI voice receptionist and how does it work?

An AI voice receptionist is an AI agent that answers your business phone with a natural voice, 24 hours a day. It understands what the caller says, answers questions with your approved information, books appointments on your calendar, transfers to a person when needed and emails you a summary of every call. The VARGERSOFT AI Voice Receptionist works in English and Spanish and costs $1,950 to set up and $349 a month with 500 minutes included.

Last reviewed: November 17, 2026 · VARGERSOFT

How an AI voice agent answers a call

  • The call rings to your number, or to a new number, and the agent picks up on the first ring.
  • Speech recognition turns what the caller says into text in real time.
  • The AI decides the answer using only the script and information you approved.
  • A natural voice speaks the answer back, in the caller's language.
  • It books, transfers or takes a message, then emails you the summary.

AI voice receptionist vs. phone menu (IVR)

A phone menu makes callers press numbers and often ends in voicemail. A voice agent holds a real conversation: the caller just says what they need, and the agent solves it or routes it. That is why callers hang up less.

Which businesses use one

Any business where a missed call is a lost sale: home services like plumbing, HVAC and roofing; dental and medical offices; law firms; auto and truck repair; salons; real estate. It works the same in any state, because it lives on the phone line, not in a building.

What to check before you choose one

  • Does the voice sound natural, and can you hear a sample call first?
  • Does it speak Spanish natively, not a translated script?
  • Does it connect to your calendar and transfer to a person?
  • Is it month to month, and how much is each extra minute?
