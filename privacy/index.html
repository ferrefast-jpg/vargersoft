Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who we are

VARGERSOFT is the software division of VARGER Group LLC, a Texas limited liability company based in Conroe, Texas, United States. This policy covers the website vargersoft.com and the services we sell through it. Each app we publish has its own privacy policy on its own website.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you send us: name, email, company, phone and the content of your message.
  • Business information you give us to set up a service, such as your services, prices, hours and calendar access.
  • Payment records. Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Basic technical data from your visit, such as pages viewed and browser type.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and provide the service you asked for.
  • To bill you and keep the records the law requires.
  • To keep the website and services working and secure.

Data of your customers

When we run an AI agent for your business, it handles information about your own customers, such as names, phone numbers and call summaries. That information belongs to you. We process it only to deliver your service, and you can ask for an export or deletion at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the providers that help us deliver the service, such as hosting, payment processing, telephony, messaging and AI model providers, and when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep information for as long as you are a customer and for the period needed to meet legal and accounting duties. Contact messages that do not become a project are deleted within 24 months.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information by writing to sales@vargersoft.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, the CCPA or the GDPR. We answer every request within 30 days.

Children

This website and our business services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date. Questions: sales@vargersoft.com.
